## Notes

- 36 components across 3 anchors (individual, community, institution).
- Top of map: lived experience and daily-life touchpoints (identity, safety, pronouns, presentation). Middle: civil-society, media, policy. Bottom: clinical infrastructure and deep social-ontological categories (biological sex, binary norms) on which everything else rests.
- Right side (commodity): things broadly taken-for-granted in March 2022 — marriage equality in most Western jurisdictions, anti-discrimination law on sex, binary social defaults, HRT as established medicine, faith communities as long-settled institutions.
- Left side (genesis/custom): the contested frontier — self-ID reform, youth gender medicine, puberty blockers, sports eligibility, conversion-practice bans, gender-critical advocacy as a newly mobilised counter-movement.
- Fragile dependencies: lived experience and inclusion policies depend on clinical pathways (youth services in particular) and on legal recognition — both sitting in genesis/custom built. Media discourse amplifies this fragility.
